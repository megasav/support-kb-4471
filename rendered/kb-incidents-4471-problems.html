parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Reports: 379. Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads 07/04 as 4 July, so day and month swap: “Customer booked 7 April and got an invite for 4 July.” This happens in every channel and at every tenant on file. It is the bug the #4471 page describes. Fixed: the parser now reads dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: booking-v3 prints a month-first hint and reads the day first

Reports: 33. They come in through the booking form at the tenants on booking-v3. Under the date field that form prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The swap looks the same as parser, but the parser is not involved, so the parser fix does not end it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new reports come in this way.

Intake tagged these reports by the reporter’s browser, not by the tenant’s form. Intake’s hint tag and this group do not match, so do not use the tag to triage this problem.

one-day: the date moves by one day

Reports: 5. Here day and month do not swap. The date moves by one day: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” Others say the invite says Tuesday and the calendar says Monday, that only June is one day off, and that the invite arrived before the booking was made. That points at time zones, not at the date format, so neither the parser fix nor the hint fix ends it. The desk had already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). By tenant they are filed under parser, and the desk still counts them there. The parser fix does not cover them. Still open, with no fix planned yet.
